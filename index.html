ABOUT SAEFFE

A family enterprise built on practical skills.

SAEFFE Enterprise brings together family skills in vehicle body work, painting, carpeting/upholstery and joinery. We also make wooden doors, stools, frames and other furniture products.

The name SAEFFE is formed from family names, representing family, teamwork, creativity and a shared commitment to making useful, quality work.
